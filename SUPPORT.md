# Supporting The Allsparks

FTC Dev Tools is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. Optional donations help sustain **The Allsparks** and the continued development of tools shared with the FIRST Tech Challenge community.

Donations are welcomed, never required, and never a substitute for contribution through issues, pull requests, documentation, or testing.

## Why donate?

Financial support helps The Allsparks continue educational robotics work and maintain community tooling. Depending on season needs, donations may help with areas such as:

- FTC registration and competition fees
- field equipment and robot hardware
- testing hardware, including Control Hub and Driver Hub validation
- development machines and CI infrastructure
- documentation, educational materials, and outreach
- support for rookie teams
- continued FTC Dev Tools development

Not every dollar goes exclusively toward software. Team education, competition participation, and shared tooling all depend on sustainable support.

## Fiscal sponsorship

The Allsparks is **not** an independently incorporated nonprofit.

The Allsparks is fiscally sponsored through **Hack Club’s HCB (Hack Club Bank)**, operated by **The Hack Foundation**, a recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Donations are processed through our nonprofit fiscal sponsor. Please rely on the official donation page and receipt for tax and legal information.

**Official donation page:**

[https://hcb.hackclub.com/donations/start/the-allsparks](https://hcb.hackclub.com/donations/start/the-allsparks)

Do not send money through personal payment apps, informal transfers, or unofficial links associated with this repository.

## Relationship to FTC Dev Tools

FTC Dev Tools remains free forever.

Donations do **not** provide:

- priority support
- roadmap influence
- ownership of the project or its trademarks
- governance authority
- sponsorship of individual contributors as maintainers
- access to private Allsparks repositories
- consulting or custom development contracts
- paid feature placement

Feature requests and pull requests are evaluated using the same project standards regardless of donor status. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Other ways to help

If you prefer not to donate, or want to help in addition to donating:

- report bugs
- submit pull requests
- improve documentation
- test on FTC hardware
- help rookie teams
- answer questions in Discussions (when enabled)
- improve translations
- review pull requests
- share the project with other teams

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
